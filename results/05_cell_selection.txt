   WHY THIS SCHEME, in one place:
   * race carries the real human spread (Phase 0: 27.4%-38.2% Yes across race,
     versus ~3 points across gender and ~3 across age), so RQ2 has something to
     detect. A gender x age scheme would ask the model to reproduce differences
     the humans barely show.
   * crossing with gender ties Phase 2 to Phase 1: gender is the axis the
     published filter is actually biased on (men removed at 3.4x, p=0.026).
   * it is a balanced 2x3 factorial, so main effects and the interaction are
     separable, which a ragged 6-cell pick would not allow.
   * smallest cell is 10 raters. Thin, and reported as such everywhere.

   COST: Latine/x and Multiracial raters are excluded from the persona
   comparison. Multiracial has the HIGHEST %Yes in the pool (38.2), so the
   chosen cells span a narrower range than the full pool does. This understates
   human diversity and must be stated as a limitation, not buried. Both groups
   remain in the Phase 3 human conditions, which use all 123 raters.
