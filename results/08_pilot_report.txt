   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   *** VERDICT: PERSONA CONDITIONING NOT DEMONSTRATED ***

   The output does vary (2/15 between-cell differences), but it
   varies AT LEAST AS MUCH between two personas from the SAME cell
   (4/15). Two personas sharing race and gender cannot differ
   BECAUSE of race or gender, so that variation is noise. A demographic
   effect that does not exceed its own noise floor has not been shown.

   This is NOT the same as the model being insensitive - the output is not
   frozen. It is that the variation present is not attributable to the
   manipulation. On 15 items with one sample each, the two cannot be
   separated, so this is UNRESOLVED rather than negative.

   DO NOT read this as 'personas fail to reproduce human diversity'. The
   design cannot support that claim yet.

   Decisive next step, cheap: re-run ONE persona on the SAME items several
   times to measure the pure sampling noise floor directly, then test whether
   the between-cell difference exceeds it. Until that is done, neither a
   positive nor a negative persona finding is supportable.
